mixpilot privacy policy

mixpilot is used only by its owner, on the owner's own YouTube channel.

  • It uses Google sign-in to read the owner's channel, videos and analytics, and to upload and update the owner's own videos.
  • Data it reads is stored only in the owner's own computer and cloud account, to run the tool.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No other users' data is collected.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
